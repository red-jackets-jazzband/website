## Instuderen
- [Bourbon Street Parade](/songs/bourbon_street_parade.abc)
  > Intro: trumpet signal
  > - Collective, with repeat
  > - Vocals with repeat, trombone riffs behind the vocals
  > - 1x collective, solos get riffs straight away (Kees Tijssen 1)
- [The way I ride](/songs/all_the_girls.abc)
  > Intro: snare; after 4 bars washboard and bass drum join in.
  > Sousaphone and banjos come in together, after the washboard and bass drum???
  > - A as written, with repeats, trombone tailgate
  > - Trumpet solo
  > - Vocals
  > - 2x solo, the 2nd solo with a "staircase" (trappetje)
  > - Vocals with ending and trombone tailgate
- [Isle of Capri](/songs/isle_of_capri.abc)
  > A-A-B-A, up-tempo with the B in swing.
  > Drums tick (ride/hi-hat).
  > B is the bridge.
  > 2nd trumpet: harmony notes, and long notes under the bridge.
  > Trombone fills in; sousaphone, washboard and banjos hold the rhythm tight and are the engine of the tune.
  > Riff = Kees Tijssen 5, first part as written by Kees Tijssen. Riff under all the A's; under the B long notes are played.
- [Bellamina](/songs/bellamina.abc)
  > Read from top left to bottom right. Drums tick, lead plays short, 2nd trumpet plays between the lines, sousaphone-washboard-banjos swing.
  > - 2x collective
  > - Vocals, 2x
  > - 1x collective
  > - Solos, riffs with long notes underneath
  > - Vocals, 2x, with long notes underneath the 2nd time
  > - 2x collective; in the last 4 bars of the 2nd collective the lead trumpet plays 8va, straight out
- [Gotta woman](/songs/gotta_woman.abc)
  > Shuffle; call and response between trumpets and trombone, raw.
- [Lady be good](/songs/lady_be_good.abc)
  > A-B swing, banjos pump manouche-style.
  > 2nd trumpet plays arpeggios.
  > - Drums count off
  > - 2x collective
  > - Vocals
  > - Solos with riff under the B, arpeggios
  > - Vocals
  > - Collective
  > - Ending straight out, chromatic?
- [Dippermouth Blues](/songs/dippermouth_blues.abc)
  > - Intro: lead trumpet with rhythm section
  > - Collective A with repeat, trombone fills the gaps
  > - Clarinet solo with the riff as written (C)
  > - Collective A without repeat
  > - Sax or trombone solo without riff, with banjo, but audience interaction
  > - Trumpet solo (2x 12 bars) without riff, with banjo, audience
  > - Trumpet solo (1x 12 bars), the 12th bar with a pickup where drums call "oh play that thing"?
  > - Collective A with repeat; the intro is used as outro, with rhythm section
